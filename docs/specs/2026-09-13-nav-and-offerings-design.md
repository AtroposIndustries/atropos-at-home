# Atropos — navigation and offerings rework

**Date:** 2026-09-13
**Branch:** `restructure-services`
**Status:** implemented on `restructure-services`, awaiting merge
**Supersedes in part:** `2026-09-08-service-consolidation-design.md` (its service
list, route table, nav content and stub map; its decisions 2, 4 and 5 stand)

## Context

The consolidation shipped three residential and four commercial services with
descriptive sub-lines under every dropdown item. Most visitors are on phones,
where hover does not exist and the sub-lines are noise in a drawer. At the
same time two offerings were missing outright — residential audio visual
outside the theatre, and unified communications for workplaces — and two
commercial labels no longer said what the pages sell.

This document reorders the offering, adds the two pages, renames two more,
and strips the dropdowns to plain labels.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|----------|-----|
| 1 | Dropdown items are plain labels — no `desc`, no "All … services" row | Hover does not exist on phones; the parent label already links to the landing. Shorter drawers are the point |
| 2 | Residential gains **Audio Visual**; "Whole-home Sound" moves out of Home Theatre into it | Multi-room audio, living-area displays and outdoor AV are a different conversation from a cinema room. Folding audio into theatre (2026-09-08, decision 1) under-served it |
| 3 | Commercial gains **Unified Communications**; "Meeting Rooms & Conferencing" moves out of Audio Visual into it | Meeting rooms are a UC sale, not a signage sale. Telephony appears as **integration with the client's existing platform**, never as something Atropos supplies |
| 4 | Commercial Audio Visual becomes **Digital Signage & Audio Visual** at a new slug; Automation & Smart Lighting becomes **Automation** at a new slug | Labels changed enough that the old slugs would mislead. URLs follow labels; stubs cover the old paths. This reverses the consolidation's "surviving slugs keep URLs" for these two pages only |
| 5 | `support/` and `networks/` keep their slugs | Not renamed pages. Managed Services was not asked about; `networks/` already matches "Network" |
| 6 | Order is fixed, not alphabetical | Residential: Home Theatre, Audio Visual, Network & Wi-Fi, Smart Home Automation. Commercial: Unified Communications, Digital Signage & Audio Visual, Network & Wi-Fi, Managed Services, Automation |

## Copy

**New pages follow the existing shape** — hero, intro, features ending in
Aftercare, CTA, contact form.

**Residential Audio Visual:** Multi-room Audio · Living-area Displays ·
Outdoor Audio & Screens · Sources & Streaming · Architectural Speakers · One
Control Surface · Coordinated With Your Build · Aftercare.

**Unified Communications:** Meeting Rooms · Video Conferencing (Teams and Zoom
Rooms) · Hybrid & BYOD · Room Booking & Scheduling · Telephony Integration ·
Standardised Rooms Across Sites · Aftercare.

**Digital Signage & Audio Visual:** Digital Signage & Displays · Video Walls &
Large Format · Content Scheduling · Zoned Audio & Paging · Acoustics ·
Coordinated With Your Build · Aftercare.

**Standing rules, unchanged:** no commercial track record claimed or implied,
no adverbs of frequency about Atropos's own commercial work, no invented SLA
or uptime figures, no unverifiable standards or legal claims, no individuals,
no smallness signals, brand is "Atropos". Security and telephony appear only
as integration with what the client already has. Hardware and platform brands
may be named (per the 2026-09 tone decision) without implying exclusivity.

**Homepage OFFERINGS blurbs** are rewritten to list the new offerings. The
commercial blurb currently names "security" — a violation of the standing
no-security rule — and is corrected here.
